Signalling: Primary signals (P) protect occupied block sections with Stop and Clear aspects. Distant signals (D) preview the upcoming Primary signal (Expect Stop or Expect Clear). Passing a Stop signal trips automatic emergency brake enforcement. Click any signal or junction switch to command routes.

Advanced Systems: Deadman vigilance control system. When active, you must confirm alertness by pressing Q or clicking the cab pedal at least once every 30 seconds while driving. If unacknowledged, a visual alert triggers at 30s, followed by an urgent alarm at 32.5s and emergency braking enforcement at 35s.
